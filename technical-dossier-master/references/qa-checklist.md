# Mandatory quality gates

## Gate A: Research integrity
- [ ] Technical scope, geographical and temporal bounds explicit
- [ ] Every key dimension/flow/efficiency/temperature/model and causal explanation recorded in ledger
- [ ] Claims are tied to precise source locations, not only generic homepages
- [ ] Primary claims read directly or conspicuously labeled indirect
- [ ] Source year, model revision, design/as-built/operation stages distinguished
- [ ] Conflicting figures preserved with definitions and context
- [ ] Derived values reproducible with calculations, units and assumptions
- [ ] Missing answers clearly reported (no fabricated bridging)
- [ ] Standards and patents distinguished from evidence of implemented equipment

## Gate B: Figure integrity
- [ ] Every figure has source/owner, location, year if known and status
- [ ] Original vs redraw vs generated schematic conspicuously distinguished
- [ ] Charts/technical legends readable and not misleadingly cropped
- [ ] Source links actually point to source where possible
- [ ] Reuse rights considered; copyrighted images not falsely claimed as owned

## Gate C: Document integrity
- [ ] Title, date, research method and limitations present
- [ ] Full PDF rendered, all pages inspected for blank areas and overflow
- [ ] Chinese font embedded or correctly displayed; symbols intact
- [ ] Images high enough resolution; captions and sources readable
- [ ] Figure numbers, page numbers, links and bibliography consistent
- [ ] No placeholder claims, broken links or orphaned figure captions
- [ ] No excessive white space caused by rigid per-chapter pagination

## Final audit
Write a short audit result: PASS / CONDITIONAL PASS / FAIL. FAIL means do not market as verified technical dossier. Conditional pass must explain exactly what could not be verified.
