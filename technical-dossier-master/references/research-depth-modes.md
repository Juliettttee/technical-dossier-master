# Research depth modes / 研究深度模式

This file controls effort, deliverables and upgrade gates. It is a budget discipline, not a relaxation of factual honesty. Every mode must preserve source fidelity, label uncertainty and refuse fabricated precision.

Clickable links are required in every mode. Light mode may use fewer sources, but each online source it uses must still be a visible descriptive link placed near the supported claim and recorded in the compact source list.

## Opening prompt

Use a compact prompt in the user's language:

> 先选研究深度：
>
> 1. 轻量（推荐起步）：快速判断，少量关键来源，简短结论，不默认生成 PDF。
> 2. 中量：面向决策的系统比较，核查关键参数，PDF 可选。
> 3. 专业：完整技术档案、证据台账、对抗审计、图表和 PDF 全量 QA。
>
> 如果后面发现不够，可以在保留已有工作的基础上升级。请选择 1/2/3。

Do not add discovery questions to this opening message. Ask the Adaptive Discovery Interview only after the user selects a mode.

## Mode 1 - Light / 轻量

Purpose: establish whether the topic deserves deeper work and support an early directional decision.

Default boundaries:

- One tightly framed decision or comparison.
- Prefer 3-6 decisive sources, not an arbitrary source-count target.
- Read only the most relevant Resource Pack files or exact sections.
- One research pass plus one contradiction/quality check.
- Deliver a concise answer or approximately 1-3 pages of content.
- A compact source list is sufficient; do not create full evidence, trace, figure and claim-audit files unless a specific risk demands one.
- No PDF unless explicitly requested. If requested, produce a short PDF after outline approval and use the same render-and-inspect requirement.
- No decorative illustrations; include a figure only when it materially improves a decision.

Stop after answering the defined decision. Offer an upgrade only for named unresolved issues.

## Mode 2 - Standard / 中量

Purpose: support a real engineering, procurement, product or management decision without producing a full due-diligence dossier.

Default boundaries:

- Several named sub-questions and a domestic/international split when relevant.
- Targeted primary-source research, normally 8-15 useful sources, stopping when further retrieval is unlikely to change the decision.
- Inspect relevant Resource Pack sections on demand; do not ingest entire libraries.
- Maintain a key-claim evidence table and conflict log. Full research trace and claim audit are optional unless risk is high.
- Deliver approximately 6-12 pages or an equivalent structured Markdown report.
- PDF is optional and requires outline approval plus visual QA.
- Use 1-3 decision-critical original or analytical figures, not decorative page filling.

## Mode 3 - Professional / 专业

Purpose: create an auditable technical dossier for due diligence, product development, formal client communication or high-consequence decisions.

Apply the complete workflow in `SKILL.md`:

- Multi-pass primary-source research and independent-source mapping.
- Full source register, evidence ledger, research trace, claim audit, research gaps and figure register.
- Detailed calculations with assumptions and reproducibility.
- Standards and patent boundaries where relevant.
- Publication-quality PDF after mandatory outline approval.
- Full render, visual QA and cold-reader review.

Do not choose Professional merely because the topic is technical. It is justified by the decision consequence, audit requirement or explicit user request.

## Upgrade rules

- Never upgrade silently.
- Preserve and reuse work already completed in the lower mode.
- State exactly what the upgrade adds, such as more primary sources, a full ledger, supplier comparison, standards mapping or PDF QA.
- A format request is not a depth upgrade. A Light PDF remains a short evidence review; a Professional PDF is a full dossier.
- If the user asks for certification, legal/FTO, safety-critical or other high-assurance conclusions, explain why Light or Standard cannot support that assurance and request an explicit upgrade or narrow the claim.

## Downgrade rules

The user may downgrade at any time. Stop additional retrieval, preserve completed artifacts, and produce the smallest useful deliverable from verified work already available.
