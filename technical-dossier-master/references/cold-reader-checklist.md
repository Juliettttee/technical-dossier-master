# Cold-reader and PDF QA

- Can a reader identify the research question, important findings, unresolved unknowns, and whether the report supports an actual engineering decision?
- Do caption and title clearly distinguish project-specific evidence, generic reference design, simulation, preliminary proposal, and analytical inference?
- Are abbreviations, reference conditions, and technical units understandable without chat history?
- Are headline conclusions faithfully supported by cited sources and in-document technical evidence?
- Does the TOC match all pages and are figure/page references clickable and correct?
- Are source images legible at printed A4 size, with intact axes, scales and legends?
- Are there oversized blank regions, clipped labels, broken Chinese glyphs, dangling captions or hyperlinks to unrelated landing pages?
- If the reader disagrees, can they trace each decisive parameter to an exact document, section, page, or figure?
